# SafeWord Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [DATE]

SafeWord is a team word-guessing game for workplace safety training. This
policy explains what information the app handles and why.

We have tried to keep this short and readable rather than exhaustive and
lawyerly, because the honest answer is that SafeWord collects very little.

## The short version

SafeWord has no player accounts, no sign-up, and no advertising. We do not
ask for your name, email, or any personal details to play. We do not sell or
share information with anyone.

## What the app handles

### Team names
When you start a game, you type in team names. These are stored temporarily
so the game can display scores. If you play a multiplayer game with a room
code, team names are stored on our server for the duration of that game and
deleted when the game ends.

Team names are free-text, so please avoid entering real full names or other
personal information if you would rather they not be stored at all.

### Game room codes
Multiplayer games use a short code so two devices can share a game. The room
record holds only the game state: team names, scores, whose turn it is, and
which categories were selected. It is deleted when the game finishes.

### Usage statistics
We record anonymous, aggregate counts about how the game is used — for
example, how often each card category is chosen, how many games are started
and completed, and which score targets are most popular. This helps us decide
which card content to expand.

These records contain no personal information, no accounts, no device
identifiers, and no IP addresses. They cannot be traced back to an individual
player or game.

### Administrator accounts
People who manage card content sign in with an email address and password.
This applies only to SafeWord staff, not to players. These credentials are
handled by our database provider (Supabase) and are used solely for
authentication.

### Information handled automatically by our hosting providers
Like any website, our hosting provider records standard server logs
(including IP addresses) for security and reliability purposes. We do not use
these logs to identify or track individual players.

## Cookies and local storage

SafeWord does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The app may store small
amounts of data in your browser to keep the game working while you play, and
to allow the app to be installed and used offline as a Progressive Web App.

## Service providers

SafeWord is built on the following third-party services, each of which
processes data on our behalf:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Supabase | Card content database, usage statistics, admin authentication |
| Firebase (Google) | Temporary multiplayer game room synchronization |
| Netlify | Website hosting |

## Children

SafeWord is a workplace safety training tool intended for adults in an
occupational setting. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly
collect information from children.

## Data retention

Multiplayer game rooms are deleted when a game ends. Anonymous usage counts
are retained indefinitely because they contain no personal information.
Administrator accounts are retained until the account is removed.

## Your choices

Because SafeWord does not collect personal information from players, there is
generally nothing tied to you for us to look up, correct, or delete. If you
have a question or a specific concern, contact us at the address below and we
will do our best to help.
