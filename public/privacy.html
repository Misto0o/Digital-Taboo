Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9/17/2026

SafeWord has no player accounts, no sign-up, and no advertising. We don't ask for your name, email, or any personal details to play, and we don't sell or share information with anyone.

What the app handles

Team names

When you start a game, you type in team names. These are stored temporarily so the game can display scores. In a multiplayer game, team names are stored on our server for the duration of that game and deleted when it ends. Because this is a free-text field, we'd suggest avoiding real full names if you'd rather they not be stored at all.

Game room codes

Multiplayer games use a short code so two devices can share a game. The room record holds only game state — team names, scores, whose turn it is, and which categories were selected. It's deleted when the game finishes.

Usage statistics

We record anonymous, aggregate counts about how the game is used, such as how often each card category is chosen and how many games are started and completed. This helps us decide which card content to expand. These records contain no personal information, no accounts, no device identifiers, and no IP addresses, and can't be traced back to an individual player or game.

Administrator accounts

People who manage card content sign in with an email address and password. This applies only to SafeWord staff, not to players, and is used solely for authentication.

Hosting provider logs

Like any website, our hosting provider records standard server logs (including IP addresses) for security and reliability. We don't use these logs to identify or track individual players.

Cookies and local storage

SafeWord doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. The app may store small amounts of data in your browser to keep the game working while you play, and to allow it to be installed and used offline.

Children

SafeWord is a workplace safety training tool intended for adults in an occupational setting. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Data retention

Multiplayer game rooms are deleted when a game ends. Anonymous usage counts are retained indefinitely because they contain no personal information. Administrator accounts are retained until removed.

Your choices

Because SafeWord doesn't collect personal information from players, there's generally nothing tied to you for us to look up, correct, or delete. If you have a question or concern, contact us and we'll do our best to help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date above and post the revised version here.
